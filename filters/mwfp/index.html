GE Appliances MWFP refrigerator water filter

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

GE Appliances says the MWF filter has been replaced by the MWFP, and that the label on the MWFP reads MWF.

MWFP is the improved replacement for MWF. GE also groups MWF with GWF and HWF as the SmartWater cartridge. GSWF and MSWF are a cylindrical filter and GE says they are not interchangeable with MWF or GWF.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

How to find the model number

Use the model number the manufacturer asks for on its filter finder, and the part number printed on the cartridge you take out. The pages we checked do not share one sentence that locates the label on every refrigerator. If the manual says the label is inside the fridge or on the door frame, follow that wording for your model. Confirm the part on the old filter and in the manual before you buy. This is a guide, not a repair service. Filters sold as compatible are not listed here unless the manufacturer itself says they are approved.

Filter light

The SmartWater article we checked does not publish a button sequence for the filter light. Use the owner's manual for your model.

GE says the current filter for this family is the MWFP, or the XWF on select models made after June 2018, and that XWF was replaced by XWFE in 2020. MWFP and XWFE are different cartridges. Do not force one into the other's head.
