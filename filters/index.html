Which water filter fits this refrigerator?

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Start with the part number on the filter you take out, and the refrigerator model number. The cards below are official cartridges we verified. A model page is published only when the manufacturer lists that model.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Find the model number and the part number

Take the old cartridge out and read the number printed on it. Then find the refrigerator model. Whirlpool, KitchenAid, and GE Appliances each ask for that model on their filter pages. We do not invent a location. If your manual says the label is inside the fresh food compartment or on the door frame, use that spot for your model and ignore a guess from a different brand.

These model pages are not in this version, because the official page we opened did not list that exact model: Whirlpool WRF535SWHZ, GE GSS25GSHSS, Samsung RF28R7351SR, LG LFXS26973S, Frigidaire FG4H2272UF, and KitchenAid KRFC302ESS. The same gap covers EDR2RXD1, EDR3RXD1, EDR4RXD1, HAF-CIN, HAF-QIN, LT1000P, LT800P, and EPTWFU01 until their current official pages are checked line by line.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the right refrigerator water filter?

Read the part number on the cartridge you remove, and the model number the manufacturer asks for. Then open that manufacturer's filter page. A chart on another website is not a source we use.

Where is the model number?

The official filter pages ask you to enter the model. They do not all describe the same label location. Use the wording in your manual, which is often on a label inside the refrigerator or on the door frame.

Do you list compatible aftermarket filters?

No. A third party filter is listed only if the manufacturer itself says it is approved. None of the pages in this version do that.
