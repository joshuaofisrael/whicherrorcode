Refrigerator filter light reset

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Only the reset behavior published by the manufacturer is on this page. A button sequence from a video or another brand is not included.

GE Appliances XWFE

After you install a new XWFE filter, GE says the Filter Status LED flashes 3 times and the Filter Status indicator turns off. If the filter is dry, fill it with water first. A wet filter can shut the water valve. The bypass plug with RFID is WR17X33825, and GE says that plug does not detect leaks.

XWFE details

GE Appliances MWFP and MWF

GE's SmartWater article explains which cartridge to buy. It does not print a filter light reset. Use the owner's manual for your model. MWFP · MWF

Whirlpool EDR1RXD1

The everydrop product page tells you to replace the filter every 6 months or 200 gallons. It does not print a light reset that covers every Whirlpool, Maytag, Amana, KitchenAid, and JennAir refrigerator. Use the manual. EDR1RXD1

Samsung, LG, and Frigidaire

Reset steps for HAF-CIN, HAF-QIN, LT1000P, LT800P, and EPTWFU01 are not on this page yet. We will add them only after the official support page for that cartridge is checked.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset a GE XWFE filter light?

GE says a new XWFE filter makes the Filter Status LED flash 3 times and then turn off. That page does not add a button sequence.

How do I reset an MWFP or EDR1RXD1 filter light?

The GE SmartWater article and the Whirlpool EDR1RXD1 page we checked do not publish one button sequence for every model. Use the owner's manual.

What if the light stays on?

Confirm the cartridge is the one the manual names, and that it is seated. On XWFE models, GE says a wrong or non GE filter can show ERR and shut the water valve. Then use the manual or the manufacturer.
