Oven error codes by brand

By Which Error Code. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

The letters do not travel. Whirlpool F1 E1 names the oven control or its wiring. GE's F0, F1, F6, and F7 page names the keypanel, and GE says it often needs to be replaced. Samsung does not publish that F list. Start with the name on the oven, then the code.

Three F-looking codes that are not the same fault

Whirlpool's range pages were opened 21 Nov 2026. F2 E1 asks whether the cooktop grates sit level, and only then names the keypad or the control. GE's F2, on the page last read 18 Nov 2026, says the oven temperature exceeded a preset threshold. The GE owner step is a breaker reset of 1 to 2 minutes, then service if it returns. Samsung's C-24, last read 16 Nov 2026, is a temperature that rises faster than normal. Samsung says that is generally a blocked vent on the back of the cooktop, and that you can clear it while cooking by opening and closing the door a few times.

The sensor words diverge the same way. Whirlpool F3 E1 names the oven temperature sensor, the control, or the wiring, and so do F1 and F2, which do not look like sensor codes. GE's F3 and F4 share one line: the oven sensor usually needs to be replaced. A Whirlpool reset does not become a GE parts instruction, and a GE keypanel note does not become a Whirlpool repair.

  • GE oven codes

  • Samsung oven codes

    C-24, the door plunger, bAd LinE, and the other codes last read 16 Nov 2026.

  • Whirlpool F2 E1

    The grate check. Do not use it on a GE oven.

Door displays

Whirlpool's F5, F9-1, and F7 E1 pages name a latch or a door switch and then give the 1 minute breaker reset. They do not publish the motorized-switch steps on GE Loc Door. Samsung's door code is the round black plunger, which should stick out about three quarters of an inch when the door is open. Use the page that matches the brand. Do not force a door that is locked after self-clean.

Frequently asked questions

Which oven brand should I open?

Open the brand printed on the oven. Whirlpool F1 E1 names the oven control. GE's F1 names the keypanel. Samsung does not use that F list.

Does F2 mean the same thing on Whirlpool and GE?

No. Whirlpool F2 E1 starts with the cooktop grates. GE's F2 page says the oven temperature exceeded a preset threshold. Samsung's fast temperature code is C-24, and Samsung says it is generally a blocked vent.

Which checks are safe on an oven?

Only the checks on that brand's page. Unplug the appliance or switch off the breaker before you move it. Shut the gas off before any step the manufacturer did not publish.
