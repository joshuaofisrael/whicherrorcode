Washer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Pick the brand printed on the washer. The letters are not a shared code. On the pages we have checked, Samsung 4C means water is not entering, LG OE means the washer cannot drain, and Whirlpool F9 E1 means the drain is taking too long.

Rechecked 20 Nov 2026: Whirlpool drain codes

Whirlpool's front load F9 E1 page still calls that code a long drain. The hose must not extend more than 4.5 inches (113 mm) into the standpipe or laundry tub, because drained water can siphon back. The maximum height on that page is 96 inches from the bottom of the washer. Do not tape the hose into the pipe. There has to be an air gap. On models that have one, the drain pump filter is behind the bottom dispenser drawer, and the door seal has small drain holes that clog. Some top load displays show the same alert as drn. The full checks are on the F9 E1 page.

Whirlpool's F21 and F02 page is a second article in the same family: drain time longer than expected. Straighten a kinked or clogged hose, use only HE detergent, and press Pause/Cancel. If that does not clear it, unplug the washer for 5 minutes, plug it back in, reselect the cycle, and press Start. That page does not repeat the 4.5 inch measurement. If the code stays, Whirlpool says to call for service. Details are on the F21 page.

Washer codes to open next

  • Whirlpool washer F9 E1

    Long drain: 4.5 inch hose limit, air gap, and the 96 inch height on Whirlpool's front load page.

  • Whirlpool washer F21

    Drain time longer than expected. Pause/Cancel, then a 5 minute unplug, and HE detergent only.

  • Samsung washer 4C

    Water is not entering. Hose match, open valves, and the 6 to 8 inch drain-hose depth Samsung states.

  • LG washer OE

    The washer cannot drain. LG's spin-only listening check comes before the pump filter.

LG OE is the page that tells you to run a spin-only cycle and listen for the pump before you open the filter. GE IE adds a timer the others do not: when the message starts, the washer locks the controls for 4 minutes, and unplugging starts that timer over. Bosch E17 is a blocked inlet filter or low pressure, and Bosch says the AquaStop hose contains an electric valve, so do not immerse it. Maytag LF is a slow fill, and Maytag says both the hot and the cold faucets have to be open. LG's other appliances use a different list again. An oven code, including the ones on the oven hub, is not a washer code.

Frequently asked questions

Which brand should I pick for a washer code?

Use the brand name on the washer. Samsung 4C is a fill code, LG OE is a drain code, and Whirlpool F9 E1 is a long drain. The same letters on another brand are a different page.

Are Whirlpool F9 E1 and F21 the same drain problem?

They are related, and Whirlpool publishes them on two pages. F9 E1 is a long drain, with the hose no more than 4.5 inches into the standpipe and a 96 inch maximum height on the front load page. F21, also shown as F02, is drain time longer than expected. That page adds HE detergent and a Pause/Cancel or 5 minute unplug. It does not repeat the 4.5 inch measurement.

Which checks are safe on a washer?

Only the checks written on that brand's page. Unplug the washer or switch off the breaker before you move it or open a panel. Let hot drain water cool before you open a pump filter.
