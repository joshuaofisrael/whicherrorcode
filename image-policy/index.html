Image policy

Last updated 21 Nov 2026.

Images

The mark in the header, the favicon, and the social image are original graphics we made. They are not photographs of appliances and they are not brand logos.

Article photographs

A code article may show one photograph. When it does, the picture is an AI-generated image of an unbranded control panel in ordinary light. It shows no real person, no logo, and no readable brand name. The caption says AI-generated image and links to this page. A page with no photograph is text only. We do not use illustrated or glossy renderings, and we do not present the picture as a photo of a specific person's appliance.

Logos and trademarks

We do not display appliance brand logos. Names are used only to identify the product.

See copyright or contact if you believe an image uses your work.

Which Error Code explains home appliance error codes and refrigerator water filter numbers from the manufacturer pages we cite. We are independent. Names are used only to identify the appliance. Codes and part numbers change by model, so check the manual for yours.
