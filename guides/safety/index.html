Safety notes for error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Read the code, then do only the checks the manufacturer published. Unplug the appliance before you move it. Shut the gas off before you go further on a gas dryer or range. On a freestanding range, confirm the anti-tip bracket before you pull the unit out.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Electricity

Many resets on the cited pages are a short power cut at the plug or the breaker. That is a control reset, not a repair. Do not bypass a breaker or a lid lock. A washer or dishwasher that is full of water can still be dangerous after you unplug it. Let hot water cool before you open a drain filter. Samsung and Bosch both warn that drain water can still be hot.

Gas

Gas dryers and gas ranges need the supply valve shut off before any inspection past the published check. LG's gAS dryer code, for example, only tells you to confirm the valve is on and the line is not kinked. It does not tell you to open the burner. If you smell gas, do not look for the leak with a flame or a spark. Leave and call the gas utility.

Range tip-over (CPSC)

Before you slide a freestanding or slide-in range out to read a label or clear a vent, check the anti-tip bracket. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission range tip-over alert (Publication 5007) says the bracket should be fixed to the wall or floor and the range should stay held in it. CPSC also says not to leave children unsupervised in the kitchen, not to put heavy items on an open oven door, and not to use the open door as a step or to stand. If the bracket is missing, ask the range maker whether your model should have one. Some makers will send a replacement. This site does not publish gas line, sealed system, or electrical repair steps past the manufacturer checks on each code page.

Water

If a code means a leak or an overfill, shut the supply valve. Do not keep the cycle running. A filter part number is a separate question from a sensor code. Use the filter finder only for cartridges the manufacturer names.

Refrigerators

Samsung's refrigerator code page says not to unplug the refrigerator, and not to clear the code from the panel, unless the steps for that code say to. Food safety matters if the freezer is warm. Move food when a code such as GE FF says the temperature is rising.

Frequently asked questions

Should I unplug the appliance before I look up a code?

You can read the code with the appliance on. Unplug it, or switch off the breaker, before you pull it out, remove a panel, or clean a filter.

When do I shut the gas off?

Shut the gas supply off before any inspection past the checks the manufacturer published for that code. Do not open a gas valve, igniter, or gas line. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.

Does a range need an anti-tip bracket?

Yes for freestanding and slide-in ranges that ship with one. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) says to confirm the bracket is fixed to the wall or floor and that the range stays engaged in it, and not to stand on an open oven door or put heavy items on it.

The code is not on this site. What now?

Use the manual for your model or the manufacturer's support page. We would rather omit a code than guess. You can send us the model and the official page from the contact form.
