GE Appliances washer code FE

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Water has reached a critical level.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Turn off the water and the power to the washer.

When to call a technician

GE says to call service. This is not a code to keep washing through.
