GE Appliances washer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the GE Appliances washer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

IE

Water is not entering the washer. On some models the Wash and Rinse lights blink instead of a code. Also listed as H2O SUPPLY. Check the house water supply. GE asks whether one or both valves were left off after installation or a vacation. When the message starts, the washer locks the controls for 4 minutes. GE says unplugging the washer restarts that 4 minute timer. After 4 minutes, start the cycle again. If the supply valves are open and the message returns, contact service. Full page for IE.

UE

The load is unbalanced. Reposition the laundry evenly in the basket and make sure the washer is level. If a balanced, level load still will not spin, contact service. Full page for UE.

dE

The lid is open. Close the lid. GE's separate Lid message also appears if 3 cycles were started without opening the lid. Open and close the lid, then start a new cycle. If the lid is closed and dE or Lid remains, GE says to contact service. Full page for dE.

FE

Water has reached a critical level. Turn off the water and the power to the washer. GE says to call service. This is not a code to keep washing through. Full page for FE.

CAnCELEd

The washer was paused for more than 24 hours, or it stopped itself before the cycle finished. Wait out the 4 minute lockout. GE says unplugging the washer starts that timer over. Then start the cycle again. If the problem persists, GE says to contact service.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these GE Appliances washer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the GE Appliances support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does IE mean the same thing on every GE Appliances washer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a GE Appliances washer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
