GE Appliances dryer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the GE Appliances dryer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

This appliance may burn gas. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published checks.

353

On the GE Profile UltraFast Combo, 353 most often means the lint filter is clogged with fabric softener residue. GE publishes this for that combo, not as a universal code for every GE dryer. Separate the foam filter from the plastic mesh screen. Rinse the foam with warm water and clean the screen with water and a soft brush. Put the filter back and run a wash and dry cycle. GE points to the combo filter cleaning page for the full steps. If 353 returns on a clean filter, contact GE service. Do not assume a different GE dryer uses this same code. Full page for 353.

18

On the GE Profile UltraFast Combo, 18 means the pump filter needs cleaning. GE also ties it to very wet loads or longer dry times. It is not published as a universal GE dryer code. Clean the drain pump filter using the Owner's Manual or GE's combo pump filter page. Make sure the drain hose is not pushed too far down into the standpipe. If 18 returns after the filter and the hose are corrected, contact service. Full page for 18.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these GE Appliances dryer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the GE Appliances support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does 353 mean the same thing on every GE Appliances dryer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a GE Appliances dryer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
