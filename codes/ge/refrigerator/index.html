GE Appliances refrigerator error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the GE Appliances refrigerator codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

FF

Check frozen food for thawing because the temperature is rising too high. Check the food. GE says you can disconnect power for a minute or two to reset the panel. Then confirm the code is gone and the freezer is cooling. If FF returns, or food has thawed, schedule service. Full page for FF.

PF

Power to the refrigerator was interrupted. GE says a one or two minute power disconnect can clear a fault code. Confirm cooling is on after power returns. If PF keeps returning without a power cut in the house, contact service. Full page for PF.

CI

Check the icemaker. GE lists CI as a code you may be able to resolve without a repair, but the table does not add a filter part number. Check that the icemaker feels level and the water supply is on, then reset power for a minute or two. Match any filter to the manual. CI is not a filter SKU. If the icemaker still does not work, schedule service. Full page for CI.

DE

Demo mode. Lights and controls work, but cooling is disabled. Unplug the refrigerator for a minute or two, then plug it back in. If DE remains, GE says to contact them with the model number. Full page for DE.

dE

The defrost system has not operated properly in the last 24 hours. This is different from DE, Demo mode. GE uses a lowercase d. You can disconnect power for a minute or two. Do not chip ice out of the evaporator. If dE returns, schedule service. Check frozen food. Full page for dE.

CC

Check the temperature controls because the refrigerator temperature is incorrect. Check the controls, then reset power for a minute or two if the code is still flashing. If CC returns, schedule service.

tC

Not an error. tC is Turbo Cool. tF is Turbo Freeze. GE says tC can look like EC or an upside down F with a C. Also listed as tF. Read the TurboCool article GE links from the fault code page if you want to leave the setting. Do not treat it as a failure. No service call is indicated for the Turbo Cool or Turbo Freeze indicator.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these GE Appliances refrigerator codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the GE Appliances support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does FF mean the same thing on every GE Appliances refrigerator?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a GE Appliances refrigerator?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
