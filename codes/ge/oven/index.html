GE Appliances oven error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 18 Nov 2026.

These are the GE Appliances oven codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

This appliance may burn gas. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published checks.

F0

GE's fault code page gives the same meaning for F0, F1, F6, and F7: a problem with the keypanel. GE says it often means the keypanel needs to be replaced. Also listed as F1, F6, F7. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. Watch the display for a minute before you cook again to see if the code returns. If F0, F1, F6, or F7 returns after the breaker reset, GE says a technician should replace the keypanel.

F2

For an unknown reason, the oven temperature has exceeded a preset threshold. Also listed as F20. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. If F2 or F20 returns, GE says to schedule service.

F3

GE's fault code page gives the same meaning for F3 and F4: the oven sensor usually needs to be replaced. Also listed as F4. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. If F3 or F4 returns, GE says to schedule service.

F350

An issue with the upper cooling fan circuit, or an obstruction in the cooling fan. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. Check that nothing is blocking the cooling fan vents on the range or wall oven. If F350 returns, GE says to schedule service.

F5

GE's fault code page gives the same meaning for F5, F8, and FF: the electronic control usually needs to be replaced. Also listed as F8, FF. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. If F5, F8, or FF returns, GE says to schedule service. Do not open the electronic control.

F9

GE's fault code page gives the same meaning for F9 and FC: a component has failed somewhere in the door lock circuit. Also listed as FC. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. If the door is stuck locked after a self-clean cycle rather than showing F9 or FC, GE's separate Loc Door page covers that reset. If F9 or FC returns after the breaker reset, GE says to schedule service. Do not force a locked door open.

F96

GE's fault code page gives the same meaning for F96, F97, and F7X: an internal component usually needs to be replaced. Also listed as F97, F7X. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. If F96, F97, or F7X returns, GE says to schedule service.

FD

GE says this usually means the probe or the probe receptacle needs to be replaced. Turn off the circuit breaker that powers the range or wall oven for 1 to 2 minutes, then restore power. Unplug the meat probe from the receptacle if one is connected, since FD is tied to the probe circuit. If FD returns, GE says to schedule service.

Bad Line

Power is reaching the range or wall oven, but GE says it has not been wired correctly. Contact a licensed installer or electrician to correct the wiring. GE says the oven works normally once the mis-wire is fixed. This is a wiring problem, not a part failure. An electrician, not an appliance technician, corrects it.

Unlock Door

The oven door will not unlock after a self-clean cycle finished. Let the oven finish cooling. GE's self-clean door lock will not release while the oven is still hot. Contact GE if the display still shows Unlock Door once the oven has fully cooled. GE says to contact them for the self-clean door release steps for your specific model if the door stays locked once the oven is cool.

Loc Door

The display flashes LOC DOOR even though the self-clean cycle was not selected. GE publishes a separate reset for this, different from the Unlock Door message that follows an actual self-clean cycle. First check that the self-clean cycle was not started and that a Control Lockout feature in the owner's manual is not the cause. Motorized switch ovens: with the door open, find the door jamb switch (a small black post or dowel) on the oven's face. Push it in until the oven cavity light goes off, as if the door were closed. While holding it in, press Self Clean and wait 10 seconds, then press Clear/Off or Cancel as soon as the Self Clean cycle starts. Wait 10 seconds for the display to clear. Manual switch ovens: press Self Clean, close the oven door, slide the latch to lock the door, then press Clear/Off or Cancel immediately. Unlock the door afterward. GE says the control should be reset after those steps and service should not be needed. If LOC DOOR remains, schedule service. Full page for Loc Door.

LOC

Not a fault. Child Lockout is a feature on many wall oven, freestanding range, and slide-in range models that keeps the oven from being turned on by accident. Check the owner's manual for your model for the Child Lockout button sequence, since GE says it varies by model. No service call is indicated for LOC by itself; it is a feature indicator, not a fault.

ERR

There was an invalid entry when setting the oven controls. Clear the entry and set the oven controls again using valid settings for your model. No service call is indicated for ERR by itself.

OFF

The oven is too hot to start a self-clean cycle, or it is showing OFF shortly after a Bake, Broil, or self-clean cycle ended. Let the oven cool down longer, then try setting self-clean, Bake, or Broil again. No service call is indicated for OFF by itself; it clears once the oven has cooled.

Sabbath

Not a fault. The display shows a backwards C when the oven is set for Sabbath Mode. Check the owner's manual for your model to turn Sabbath Mode on or off; the steps differ between digipad controls and up/down arrow controls. No service call is indicated for the Sabbath Mode indicator.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these GE Appliances oven codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the GE Appliances support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does F0 mean the same thing on every GE Appliances oven?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a GE Appliances oven?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
