Bosch washer code E18

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: The drainage hose is blocked or kinked. Bosch warns the water may be very hot.

The same manufacturer page also lists F18.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Set the program to Off and pull the plug. Check the drain hose for kinks. Clean the hose and the siphon connection, then run Drain.
  • Clean the pump only by the steps in the owner's manual. Bosch says to drain water slowly through the pump cover and to confirm the impeller can turn. Pour about 1 quart of water into the dispenser and run Drain to check for leaks.

When to call a technician

If E18 returns after the hose and the pump are clear, contact Bosch support.

More Bosch washer codes

  • E17: The filters in the water connection or the AquaStop hose are blocked, or the water pressure is too low.
  • E32: An imbalance was detected. Bosch says the spin may not run, or it may slow down, and the washer may try several times to spread the laundry.
  • E:00-23: There is water in the base, or the appliance is leaking. Bosch writes this as E:00 and -23.
