Bosch washer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the Bosch washer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

E18

The drainage hose is blocked or kinked. Bosch warns the water may be very hot. Also listed as F18. Set the program to Off and pull the plug. Check the drain hose for kinks. Clean the hose and the siphon connection, then run Drain. Clean the pump only by the steps in the owner's manual. Bosch says to drain water slowly through the pump cover and to confirm the impeller can turn. Pour about 1 quart of water into the dispenser and run Drain to check for leaks. If E18 returns after the hose and the pump are clear, contact Bosch support. Full page for E18.

E17

The filters in the water connection or the AquaStop hose are blocked, or the water pressure is too low. Also listed as F17, F29. Turn the tap off, remove the hose from the tap, and clean the filters with a small brush. Bosch says the AquaStop device contains an electric valve, so do not immerse it in water. On a washer without AquaStop, remove the hose from the back, take the filter out with pliers, and clean it. A fully open tap should flow about 2.5 gallons (10 liters) a minute. If pressure is too low, Bosch says to contact a plumber. Full page for E17.

E32

An imbalance was detected. Bosch says the spin may not run, or it may slow down, and the washer may try several times to spread the laundry. Also listed as F32. Wash large and small items together. Close zippers and buttons on pillow and duvet covers. An occasional imbalance is the washer protecting itself. If every normal load fails to spin, contact Bosch. Full page for E32.

E:00-23

There is water in the base, or the appliance is leaking. Bosch writes this as E:00 and -23. Turn off the water tap. Bosch says to call customer support. Do not keep the washer running. Full page for E:00-23.

E16

The door is not locked properly. Also listed as E34, F16, F34. Close the door until it clicks. A full load can block the door. Check for something jammed between the door and the housing, then restart. If the door clicks shut and the code returns, contact Bosch.

E33

Detergent foam was detected. Too much detergent, or old detergent, especially on a lightly soiled load. Also listed as F33. Reduce the detergent. Use the amount on the detergent package. This is a dosing issue unless it continues with the correct dose.

E80

A fault in the drive circuit. Bosch says faults of this type can only be eliminated by an authorized technician. Contact Bosch support. Do not open the drive.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these Bosch washer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the Bosch support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does E18 mean the same thing on every Bosch washer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a Bosch washer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
