Bosch dishwasher error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Bosch's dishwasher code page was opened again on 21 Nov 2026. The change that matters is E22. It still means a blocked filter, and the same entry now walks through the drain pump cover, the drain pump hose, and the air gap or drain hose. E15 is still water in the base: turn the inflow off and contact Bosch. The other codes already on this page still match.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

E22, reread 21 Nov 2026

Bosch says the filter is where food particles collect and that it needs regular cleaning. Remove it, wash it under running water with mild soap and a soft brush, and reinstall it. Bosch links a longer filter article and a video. Those were not opened, so the steps above are the ones on the code page itself.

The same E22 entry then says the drain pump may be blocked or the cover loose. Remove the cover, clear standing water and loose debris, and replace the cover so it clicks. Check the drain pump hose for a kink or a sharp bend. For the air gap or the drain hose, turn the dishwasher off, check the air gap, disconnect the hose into a bucket, clear debris, reconnect it, and start a rinse. If E22 remains, Bosch points to a separate reset article. That article was not opened on 21 Nov 2026, so its steps are not copied here. Contact Bosch if the code is still there. Details also sit on the E22 page.

Bosch gives E24 and E25, which this site does not publish as their own pages, the pump, hose, and air gap steps without the filter wash. E23 says the drain pump is blocked, clogged, or faulty, and it does not add those steps. Use the code on your display. Do not treat every drain code as E22.

The codes that still match

E15 means the safety switch detected water in the base and leak protection has activated. Turn off the water inflow and contact Bosch. Do not keep the dishwasher running. E16 and E17 mean water is not entering, or the filling system has a problem. Check that the inlet hose is not kinked or damaged, then contact Bosch. E18 is the flow into the dishwasher: the level is too low, or water is not being pumped correctly. Bosch says to check the inlet hose and to confirm the supply is on, and it states a flow of 10 liters a minute. E09 is a heat pump internal heater fault. The published step is a 30 second breaker reset, then support. Do not open the heater. E12 is limescale in the heat pump. Bosch says to descale, and to use softening salt if the dishwasher has a softener. E14 names a flow meter failure or a fill problem and does not add an owner procedure.

E15

The safety switch detected water in the base. Leak protection has activated. Turn off the water inflow. Bosch says to contact customer support. Do not keep using the dishwasher. Full page for E15.

E22

The filter is blocked. On the same page, Bosch also lists a blocked or loose drain pump cover, a kinked drain pump hose, and a clogged air gap or drain hose. Also listed as E-22. Remove the filter and wash it under running water with mild soap and a soft brush, then reinstall it. If the drain pump is blocked or the cover is loose, remove the cover, clear standing water and loose debris, and replace the cover so it clicks shut. Check the drain pump hose for a kink or a sharp bend. Turn the dishwasher off and check the air gap. Disconnect the drain hose, place the end in a bucket, clear debris, reconnect the hose, and start a rinse cycle. If E22 remains, Bosch links a separate reset procedure and then customer support. That reset page was not opened on 21 Nov 2026, so the steps are not copied here. Do not keep running a dishwasher that will not drain. Full page for E22.

E16

Water is not flowing into the dishwasher, or the filling system has a problem. Also listed as E17. Check that the inlet hose is not kinked or damaged. Bosch says to contact support for further troubleshooting. Full page for E16.

E12

Limescale in the heat pump. Bosch says the dishwasher needs to be descaled. Use Bosch descaler. If the dishwasher has a softener, use softening salt to slow the buildup. Follow the descaler label. If E12 returns after descaling, contact Bosch.

E09

A heat pump internal heater fault. Reset power at the breaker for 30 seconds. If E09 is not resolved, Bosch says to call support. Do not open the heater.

E18

Water flow into the dishwasher is wrong. The level is too low, or water is not being pumped correctly. Check that the inlet hose is not kinked or damaged and that the water is on. Bosch says the flow rate should be 10 liters a minute. Contact Bosch if the supply is fine and E18 remains.

E14

A flow meter failure, or water is not flowing into the dishwasher. Bosch's public entry names the fault and does not add an owner procedure on the page we checked. Contact Bosch support.

Frequently asked questions

What changed on the Bosch dishwasher page?

On 21 Nov 2026 the E22 entry still starts with a blocked filter, and it also lists a blocked or loose drain pump cover, a kinked drain pump hose, and a clogged air gap or drain hose. E15, E16, E17, E18, E09, E12, and E14 still match the earlier reading.

What does Bosch E22 mean?

The filter is blocked. Wash it under running water with mild soap and a soft brush and put it back. If the pump cover is loose or the pump is blocked, clear debris and click the cover shut. Straighten the drain pump hose. Then check the air gap and the drain hose.

What does Bosch E15 mean?

The safety switch has detected water in the base, and leak protection has activated. Turn off the water inflow and contact Bosch. Do not keep using the dishwasher.
