Whirlpool F8E2: Fill Valve Connection Leak

By Which Error Code. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Whirlpool names F8E2 a fill valve electric error.

The leak look is the part that separates F8E2 from a plain power-cycle code. Do not open the valve or the control.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Whirlpool calls F8E2 a fill valve electric error, then gives one look before the power cut: take the drip tray off and inspect the inlet valve connection for leaks. That leak look is what separates this code from F1E1, which is only a power cut. Do not open the valve.

If the tray has standing water and the code is F8E4, use the F8E4 page. If the dishwasher is not detecting water at all, use H2O.

Checks Whirlpool publishes

  • Remove the drip tray and look at the inlet valve connection for leaks.
  • Then cut power for 30 seconds and restore it.
