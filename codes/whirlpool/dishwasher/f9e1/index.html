Whirlpool F9E1: Dishwasher Will Not Drain

By Which Error Code. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Long drain. The dishwasher is not draining.

Whirlpool's F9E2 page is the drain motor code. It does not tell you to wash the filter. Use F9E1 when the complaint is a long drain, and F9E2 when the display says F9E2.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

F9E1 is the long drain. F9E2 is the page Whirlpool titles as a drain motor error, and that page does not tell you to wash the filter. Use this page when the display says F9E1 or the tub is still full.

The knockout plug matters on a new install that drains into a garbage disposer. A disposer that has already been draining is not waiting on a plug. If the sink has an air gap, clear it with that air gap maker's own instructions. Whirlpool does not print those steps.

Checks Whirlpool publishes

  • Take the filter out and wash it under running water.
  • Check the drain hose from the back of the dishwasher to the sink. Straighten a kink or a pinch, and clear a clog.
  • If a garbage disposer is connected, clear food from it. On a new install, the knockout plug in the disposer inlet has to be removed or the dishwasher cannot drain.
  • If the sink uses an air gap, follow that air gap maker's instructions to take the cap off and clear a blockage. Whirlpool does not print a separate air gap procedure.
  • Unplug the dishwasher for 30 seconds, or switch its breaker off for 30 seconds, then restore power.
  • If suds are in the tub, stop and let them settle. Use detergent made for dishwashers, not dish soap.

When to call a technician

If the tub still will not drain, schedule service. Do not pull the drain motor.
