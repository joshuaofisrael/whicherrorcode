Whirlpool H2O: Dishwasher Not Detecting Water

By Which Error Code. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Meaning: The dishwasher is not detecting water.

Whirlpool also writes this as H20, F8E1.

Whirlpool spells this H2O on its own code page, H20 on the reading chart, and F8E1 or H20 on the flashing Clean light page. The three labels share one check. This is not F8E4, which is water in the drip tray.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Three labels, one check. The dedicated page says H2O. The reading chart says H20. The flashing Clean light page says F8E1 or H20. Turn the water on, take the kink out of the supply hose, and press Start. Whirlpool does not say to unplug the dishwasher for this code.

Water in the wrong place is a different code. F8E4 is the drip tray. F8E2 is a leak at the inlet valve connection. Do not empty the drip tray for H2O unless you also see water down there.

Checks Whirlpool publishes

  • Turn the water supply on.
  • Check the water supply hose for a kink.
  • When the supply is corrected, press Start. Whirlpool says the dishwasher will try to fill and resume the cycle.

Related Whirlpool dishwasher codes

  • Whirlpool F8E4: Water in the Drip Tray

    Water in the drip tray. A professional install uses different steps that leave the power on.

  • Whirlpool F9E1: Dishwasher Will Not Drain

    Long drain. Wash the filter, clear the hose, and check a new disposer's knockout plug.

  • Whirlpool F9E2: Drain Motor Error

    Drain motor code. Hose route and disposer first. Whirlpool does not say to replace the motor.

When to call a technician

Whirlpool's H2O page does not add a power cut. If water is on, the hose is straight, and H2O, H20, or F8E1 returns, schedule service. Do not take the inlet valve apart.
