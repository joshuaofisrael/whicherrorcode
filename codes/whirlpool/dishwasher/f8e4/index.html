Whirlpool F8E4: Water in the Drip Tray

By Which Error Code. Last verified 20 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Water has been detected in the drip tray.

The professionally installed sequence tells you to leave the power on. That overrides the usual unplug step for this one case.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

F8E4 is water in the drip tray, not a failure to fill. H2O means the dishwasher is not detecting water. F8E2 is a leak check at the inlet connection. Start here when the display says F8E4.

Whirlpool splits the steps by how the dishwasher was installed. If you can see water, or the unit was just installed, seat the fittings, reconnect the float, empty the tray, and press Cancel twice. If a professional installed it, Whirlpool says the opposite of a power cut: leave the power on, keep the door closed, turn the water off, and press Cancel once. The unplug line in the safety box does not apply to that second case.

Checks Whirlpool publishes

  • If the dishwasher was just installed, seat the rubber washer in each fitting. Slide the 3/4 inch (19 mm) fitting of the 90 degree elbow up to the valve and hand-tighten it, then tighten with pliers an extra 1/4 to 1/2 turn so the gasket seals. Route the fill hose out the rear left side.
  • Confirm the float switch is connected. Line the connector up with its housing and push until the locking tab is visible over the back of the connector. Keep the power cord off the motor and off the lower part of the tub.
  • If you can see water in the drip tray, empty it. Press the snap on each side of the plastic tray in toward the center and pull the tray toward you. Do not yank it. Whirlpool says the float switch wire is still attached and a hard pull can damage that wire.
  • On that path, press Cancel once to silence the alarm, then press Cancel again to clear the code.
  • If you see suds or soap foam, turn the dishwasher off and let the suds settle. Whirlpool says detergent made for dishwashers does not create suds, and dish soap does. Those suds can push water out of the tub. Use only automatic dishwasher detergent.
  • Whirlpool gives a different sequence when a professional installed the dishwasher. The drain starts and the machine will not run. Turn the water supply off. Press Cancel once to silence the alarm. Do not turn the power off, and keep the door closed.

Related Whirlpool dishwasher codes

  • Whirlpool F9E1: Dishwasher Will Not Drain

    Long drain. Wash the filter, clear the hose, and check a new disposer's knockout plug.

  • Whirlpool F9E2: Drain Motor Error

    Drain motor code. Hose route and disposer first. Whirlpool does not say to replace the motor.

  • Whirlpool H2O: Dishwasher Not Detecting Water

    Not detecting water. Same check as H20 and F8E1: supply on, hose straight, press Start.

When to call a technician

If F8E4 continues after the path that matches your install, schedule service. Do not open the control.
