Whirlpool oven code F1 E1

By Which Error Code. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Whirlpool's range page says there may be a problem with the oven control, or the associated wiring. Reset the breaker for 1 minute. If F1 E1 returns, schedule service. This is not the temperature sensor, and it is not the grate check.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

The check Whirlpool publishes

  • Turn the circuit breaker off for 1 minute, then turn it back on.
  • Watch for 1 minute. Confirm F1 E1 stays off, then confirm the oven operates.

That is the whole owner procedure on the F1 E1 page. Whirlpool then says to schedule service. Do not open the control or the wiring to see which one it is. On a gas range, shut the gas supply off before any inspection past this reset. The same control sentence, checked the same day, is on F1 E0, F1 E2, F1 E5, F1-7, F1-9, F1-E, bare F3, and bare F6. Those stay on the range table rather than as copies of this page. F1 E7 and F1 E9 are close but not the same: they name the appliance control board.

Codes people mix up with F1 E1

  • Whirlpool F2 E1

    Check the grates while the range is cool, then reset power.

  • Whirlpool F3 E1

    The sensor sentence, in the range table. It is not this page.

  • GE F0 and F1

    GE groups F0 and F1 as a keypanel fault and often a keypanel replacement.

  • Samsung C-F0

    A communication error, with a firmware note on three induction model families.

Codes that look related and are not

F2 E1 starts with the cooktop grates, and only then names the keypad, the control, or the wiring. F3 E1 names the oven temperature sensor, the control, or the wiring. So does bare F1, which is easy to confuse with F1 E1 if the display is hard to read. Write down the full code before you pick a row. GE uses F1 for the keypanel, often as a replacement, after a 1 to 2 minute breaker reset. Samsung C-F0 is a communication error, and on three induction model families Samsung publishes a SmartThings firmware update. None of those steps are on Whirlpool's F1 E1 page.

Check a different code

Same brand, different part. Then a different brand entirely, if the name on the oven is not Whirlpool.

  • F2 E1, grates then keypad
  • F3 E1, temperature sensor
  • F1 E7, appliance control board
  • GE oven codes
  • Samsung oven codes

When to call a technician

If the code returns, Whirlpool says to schedule service. Do not open the control, a fan, an element, or any wiring. On a gas range, shut the gas supply off before any inspection past this reset.

Frequently asked questions

What does Whirlpool oven F1 E1 mean?

Whirlpool's range page says there may be a problem with the oven control, or the associated wiring. The same sentence is on F1 E0, F1 E2, F1 E5, F1-7, F1-9, F1-E, F3, and F6.

What should I do for F1 E1?

Turn the breaker off for 1 minute, turn it back on, watch for 1 minute, and confirm the oven operates. Unplug the appliance before you move it. If the code returns, schedule service. Do not open the control.

Is F1 E1 the temperature sensor?

No. The sensor sentence is on F3 E1 and on several codes that do not look like F3, including F1 and F2. F1 E1 is the control sentence. F2 E1 is the grate check.
