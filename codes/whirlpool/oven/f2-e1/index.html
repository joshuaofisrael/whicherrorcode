Whirlpool oven code F2 E1

By Which Error Code. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Whirlpool's first question is whether the cooktop grates are installed properly and sit level. A grate that wobbles, checked only when the range is cool, may be in backwards. If the code remains after you switch the grates and reset power, the same page names the user interface or keypad, the control, or the wiring, and says to schedule service.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

The grate check, then the reset

  • Wait until the range is cool. Do not rock a hot grate.
  • Rock each grate corner to corner and side to side. If it wobbles, Whirlpool says it may be installed backwards. Switch the grates so they sit level.
  • Cycle power: breaker off for 1 minute, then on. Watch for 1 minute and confirm the oven operates.

F2 E0, F2 E2, F2 E3, F2 E4, F2 E5, and F2 E6 use the keypad sentence and do not ask about the grates. Those rows are on the range table. Bare F2 is a different page again: it names the oven temperature sensor, the control, or the wiring. Read the full display. F2 and F2 E1 are not the same code.

What to open if F2 E1 is not your code

  • Whirlpool F1 E1

    Oven control or wiring, with no grate check.

  • Whirlpool F2 E0

    The keypad sentence without the grate step. It lives in the range table.

  • GE oven F2

    GE's F2 is an over-temperature code, not a grate code.

    Samsung door code

    A different oven, and a plunger check rather than cooktop grates.

What this is not

F1 E1 skips the grates and names the oven control. GE's F2 says the oven temperature exceeded a preset threshold. The GE page's owner step is a 1 to 2 minute breaker reset, with service if it returns. It does not mention grates. Samsung's door code is a plunger at the door, about three quarters of an inch when the door is open. Cooktop grates do not clear a Samsung door code, and a Samsung plunger check does not clear Whirlpool F2 E1.

Check another code

F2 E1 is the grate page. The keypad-only F2 codes and the other brands are separate.

When to call a technician

If F2 E1 returns after the grates sit level and the breaker reset, schedule service. Do not open the keypad. Do not rock a grate while it is hot. On a gas range, shut the gas off before any inspection past these checks.

Frequently asked questions

What does Whirlpool oven F2 E1 mean?

Whirlpool asks first whether the cooktop grates are installed properly and level. If a cool grate wobbles, it may be in backwards. The same page then says there may be a problem with the user interface or keypad, the control, or the wiring.

What should I check before I call for F2 E1?

With the range cool, rock each grate corner to corner and side to side. Switch any grate that wobbles, then turn the breaker off for 1 minute and back on. Watch for 1 minute. Unplug the appliance before you move the range.

Is Whirlpool F2 E1 the same as GE F2?

No. GE's F2 page says the oven temperature exceeded a preset threshold, and the owner step there is a breaker reset. GE does not mention the grates.
