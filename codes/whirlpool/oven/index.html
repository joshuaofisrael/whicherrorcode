Whirlpool oven F codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 21 Nov 2026.

On the range pages Whirlpool publishes, an F code is not one fault. The owner step on nearly every page is the same: turn the breaker off for 1 minute, turn it back on, and watch for 1 minute. What changes is the part the page names. F1 E1 names the oven control. F2 E1 first asks whether the cooktop grates sit level. F3 E1 names the oven temperature sensor. If the code returns, Whirlpool says to schedule service.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

The reset, written once

Whirlpool's wording on these pages is: power the unit down by turning off the circuit breaker for one minute, power it back up, monitor the oven for one minute so the code does not display again, and verify that the oven operates. That is a control reset, not a repair. Do not open the control, the keypad, a temperature sensor, a door latch, a fan, a bake or broil element, or any wiring. If the range burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any inspection past that reset. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility. The safety notes cover the same stop point, including the anti-tip bracket before you pull a range out.

The letter does not tell you the part

F1 is a family, not a meaning. F1 E1, F1 E0, F1 E2, F1 E5, F1-7, F1-9, F1-E, F3, and F6 all say there may be a problem with the oven control or the associated wiring. F1 E7 and F1 E9 name the appliance control board instead. F03 and F04 say electronic oven control. F06 says electronic control. F0 E0 says a component of the oven control system. Treat those as different sentences, because Whirlpool wrote them that way.

The sensor sentence is a different group, and it includes codes that do not start with F3. F01, F02, F1, F1-1, F1-2, F1-3, F1-H, F1-L, F2, F3-1, F3 E0, and F3 E1 all name the oven temperature sensor, the control, or the wiring. F3 E2 adds the warming drawer sensor on a range that has one. F3 E3 names the sensor or the wiring and does not mention the control. A reader who only remembers "F3 means the sensor" will misread F3, which is the control sentence, and will miss F1 and F2, which are the sensor sentence.

GE's oven page does not match this map. GE's F0, F1, F6, and F7 share one meaning, a keypanel problem, and GE says the keypanel often needs to be replaced. GE's F3 and F4 name the oven sensor. GE's F2 means the oven temperature exceeded a preset threshold. Samsung does not use this F list at all. Samsung's C-24 is a temperature that rises faster than normal, and Samsung says that is generally a blocked vent. Use the brand printed on the appliance. The oven hub puts those three side by side. LG oven codes are a separate list, cited to LG's range error page.

Two checks that are not only the breaker

F2 E1 is the grate page. When the range is cool, rock each grate corner to corner and side to side. If it wobbles, it may be installed backwards, so switch the grates, then cycle power. The same page still names the user interface or keypad, the control, or the wiring. F2 E0 and F2 E2 through F2 E6 name that keypad sentence and skip the grates. Do not rock a hot grate.

F3 E4 and F8 are the meat probe. Both pages say to verify the probe is securely inserted in the receptacle, and both say the location may vary. Then do the breaker reset. F8 is not the cooling fan. The fan codes are F7 E5, F7 E6, F8 E0, and F8 E2. F8 E0 says the cooling fan speed is too low and mentions an airflow restriction. F8 E2 says the speed is too high and does not mention a restriction. Neither page tells you to open the fan.

Door, heat, and Wi-Fi

Latch wording is not identical, so the table keeps it. F5 names the oven door latch assembly and the door latch switches. F5 E1, F5 E2, and F5 E3 say main oven door latch assembly and the door switch. F7 E1 names only the door switch. F7 E0 names the latch assembly and does not name the switch. F9-1, F9-2, F9-3, F1-A, and F1-C name the oven door latch and the door lock switch. None of those pages publish the button sequence on the GE Loc Door page. Do not force a locked door.

F5 E5 says the oven has not reached self-clean temperature after 30 minutes, and that there may be a problem with the door hinges or the door gasket. The only owner step is still the breaker reset. Do not take the door off. FEE6 names the main cavity bake element. FEE7 names the broil element. FEE8 publishes the reset and names no part, so do not borrow the bake or broil meaning.

FC E1 says the range could not connect to the router, and that there is no link between the Wi-Fi module and the router. The page adds a router reset: if another Wi-Fi device works at the range, disconnect router power for 2 minutes, reconnect it, then disconnect the range for 2 minutes and reconnect. If the signal there is weak, Whirlpool says you can move the router, use a compatible extender, or contact the internet provider. FC E7 says the missing link is between the Wi-Fi module and the internet, and it tells you to start a cycle after the breaker reset. FC E8 is a registration error: enter the SAID number again, and select the right appliance type and model. The other FC codes on Whirlpool's index were not opened on 21 Nov 2026 and are not in the table.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Whirlpool oven F code mean?

It depends on the rest of the code. On the range pages opened 21 Nov 2026, Whirlpool gives almost every code the same owner step, a 1 minute breaker reset, and then names a different part. F1 E1 names the oven control. F2 E1 starts by asking whether the grates sit level. F3 E1 names the oven temperature sensor.

Should I replace the part Whirlpool names?

No. Those pages tell you to reset power and to schedule service if the code comes back. They do not publish a procedure for replacing a control, a sensor, a latch, a fan, or an element. Unplug the appliance or switch off the breaker before you move the range. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published reset.

Does this table cover a Whirlpool wall oven?

No. This is the freestanding and slide-in range list. Whirlpool publishes wall oven codes separately, and that list was not opened on 21 Nov 2026. Use the manual for a wall oven.
