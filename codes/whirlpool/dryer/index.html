Whirlpool dryer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the Whirlpool dryer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

This appliance may burn gas. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published checks.

F1 E1

Whirlpool's dryer page gives a power reset and does not publish a parts diagnosis for this code. Turn the dryer breaker off for 5 minutes, then on. Start a timed dry cycle and watch for 1 minute to see if the code returns. If F1 E1 reappears, schedule service. Shut the gas off before any inspection past this reset on a gas dryer. Frequently asked questions

Where do these Whirlpool dryer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the Whirlpool support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does F1 E1 mean the same thing on every Whirlpool dryer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a Whirlpool dryer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
