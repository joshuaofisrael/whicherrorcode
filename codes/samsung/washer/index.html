Samsung washer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the Samsung washer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

4C

Water is not entering the washer correctly. Also listed as 4E, nF, 1 4C. Match the hot hose to the hot inlet and the cold hose to the cold inlet. Samsung says not to use Flood Safe hoses. Open both water valves fully and leave them open while the washer runs. Samsung says the drain hose should sit 6 to 8 inches into the drain pipe and should not be kinked. Do not remove the screw that holds the drain hose to the back of the washer. If that holder is missing, Samsung says service is required. Unplug the washer or switch off its breaker for 1 minute, then restore power and start a cycle. If you check the inlet screens, turn the water valves off first, disconnect the hoses, and clean debris from the screens. Open the valves fully after you reconnect them. If the code continues, Samsung says to request service. Full page for 4C.

4C2

The cold water supply is extremely hot, usually because the supply hoses are swapped. Also listed as 4E2, CE, 14C2, nF1. Connect the cold supply to the cold inlet and the hot supply to the hot inlet. Samsung says this is typically blue to blue and red to red. Samsung says this code does not mean the washer needs service. Full page for 4C2.

5E

Water is not draining at the correct speed, or it is not draining at all. Also listed as 5C, nd, SE, SC, 1 5C. Let the water cool for about an hour if a hot cycle just ran. Samsung warns the water can still be very hot. Level the washer. Samsung says a washer that is not level may not drain correctly. Check the drain hose: 6 to 8 inches into the standpipe or sink, secured so it cannot slip farther, not airtight, not kinked, at least 18 inches high (24 inches for a wash basin), and not higher than 96 inches (35 inches for a wash basin). Samsung says not to use a drain hose extension kit, and not to remove the hose holder screw. On a front load washer, clean the pump filter. Samsung says top load washers do not have a removable pump filter. Use the user manual for the filter location on your model. Start a cycle. Samsung says the washer should drain at the beginning. If it does not, request service. Full page for 5E.

UE

The load is unbalanced. A Ur code means the washer will add water and try to balance the load again. Samsung says Ur is not a fault. Also listed as Ub, U6, Ur, 1 Ub, 1U6. Untangle and rearrange the laundry so it sits evenly in the tub. Close the door and press Start. If the code keeps returning, Samsung points owners to its washer will not spin guide and then to service. Full page for UE.

dE

The door is not detected as closed and locked. Also listed as dC, dE1, dE2, dS, d5, FL, LO, L0, dL, 1 dC. Check that the latch is fixed, that laundry is not caught in the door, and that the door is closed. If the code cannot be cleared, or the door or latch is damaged, Samsung says to request service. Full page for dE.

SUd

The washer detected too many suds. It pauses so the suds can settle, then continues. The code may flash again at the end as a reminder. Also listed as 5UD, Sd, 5d, SUdS. Use the amount and type of detergent Samsung describes in its detergent guide. This code is caused by how detergent was used. Samsung says this code does not mean the washer needs service. Full page for SUd.

1E

Moisture is where it should not be, or the water level sensor has a problem. Also listed as LE, 1C, LC, LC1, 1 LC. Look for a leak, including a small one. Check that the supply hoses and the drain hose are not kinked. Do not remove the drain hose holder screw. Run an empty cycle with no detergent or softener. If suds appear, Samsung says too much detergent or a non HE detergent was used. Keep running empty cycles until the suds stop, then see if the code returns. If there are no suds and the code continues, Samsung says to request service. Full page for 1E.

HC

Heater error. Samsung places this code in the group that has no separate troubleshooting steps. Also listed as HE, HE1, Hr, 1 HC. Power the washer off for 2 to 3 minutes, power it back on, and restart the cycle. If the code continues, Samsung says to request service. Do not open the heater. Full page for HC.

tE

Temperature sensor error. Samsung places this code in the group that has no separate troubleshooting steps. Also listed as tE1, tC. Power the washer off for 2 to 3 minutes, power it back on, and restart the cycle. If the code continues, Samsung says to request service. Full page for tE.

3E

On some models 3E is a motor error. On others it is a voltage error. Samsung says to use the manual for your model. Also listed as 3C, E3, 1 3C. Confirm the washer is on its own circuit and is not on an extension cord, which is Samsung's step for the voltage group. If the manual shows a motor error, use the basic reset: power off for 2 to 3 minutes, then restart the cycle. If the code continues, Samsung says to request service.

PF

The power supply to the washer is irregular, or power failed during a cycle. Also listed as 9C1, 9C2, 2E, 9E1, 9E2, UC. Confirm the washer is on its own circuit and is not using an extension cord. If the code continues, Samsung says to request service.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these Samsung washer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the Samsung support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does 4C mean the same thing on every Samsung washer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a Samsung washer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
