Samsung washer code 1E

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Moisture is where it should not be, or the water level sensor has a problem.

The same manufacturer page also lists LE, 1C, LC, LC1, 1 LC.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Look for a leak, including a small one.
  • Check that the supply hoses and the drain hose are not kinked. Do not remove the drain hose holder screw.
  • Run an empty cycle with no detergent or softener. If suds appear, Samsung says too much detergent or a non HE detergent was used. Keep running empty cycles until the suds stop, then see if the code returns.

When to call a technician

If there are no suds and the code continues, Samsung says to request service.

More Samsung washer codes

  • 4C: Water is not entering the washer correctly.
  • 4C2: The cold water supply is extremely hot, usually because the supply hoses are swapped.
  • 5E: Water is not draining at the correct speed, or it is not draining at all.
  • UE: The load is unbalanced. A Ur code means the washer will add water and try to balance the load again. Samsung says Ur is not a fault.
  • dE: The door is not detected as closed and locked.
  • SUd: The washer detected too many suds. It pauses so the suds can settle, then continues. The code may flash again at the end as a reminder.
