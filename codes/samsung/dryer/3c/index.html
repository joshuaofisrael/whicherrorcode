Samsung dryer code 3C

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Samsung lists 3C as a motor error, in the group without a separate repair procedure.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Power the dryer off for 2 to 3 minutes, power it back on, and restart the cycle.

When to call a technician

If the code returns, Samsung says to check the manual and then request service.

More Samsung dryer codes

  • tE: The dryer has a temperature problem, or a problem with the sensor that measures temperature.
  • HC: The dryer is getting too hot. Samsung also says some cases are the heater not getting hot enough, which on an electric dryer can be low voltage.
  • HC2: Samsung lists HC2 as a gas ignition error. It is in the group with no separate troubleshooting steps.
  • dE: The door, the latch, or the sensor that checks them is not secured.
  • Clg: These codes appear only after a Vent Blockage test. Clg or Cg means the vent is clogged. C80 or C8 means about 80 percent is clogged. C90 or C9 means over 90 percent is clogged.
