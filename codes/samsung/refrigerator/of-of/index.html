Samsung refrigerator code OF OF

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Cooling Off mode, also called Demo or Shop mode. The lights and controls can work while cooling is off. Samsung says service is not required.

The same manufacturer page also lists O FF, OFF OFF.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Samsung's refrigerator code page says not to unplug the refrigerator, and not to press buttons to hide a code, unless the steps for that code tell you to. Unplugging can make the fault harder to diagnose. The unplug step in the box above is for checks that require you to move the cabinet or open a panel.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • On many outside panels, the common method is to touch and hold the top two buttons on the left side for 5 to 8 seconds until a chime sounds. Some newer models also need the top button on the right. Samsung says to try two buttons first, and to use the manual for your model.
  • Wait and confirm the display does not return to Cooling Off mode.

When to call a technician

If the display will not leave this mode, use the manual for your model or request help. This is not a cooling failure by itself.

More Samsung refrigerator codes

  • 5E: Samsung lists 5E as a fridge defrost sensor error. It is in the common code list, and Samsung says most refrigerator codes need service.
  • 8E: Samsung lists 8E as an ice maker (freezer) sensor error.
  • 40E: Samsung lists 40E and 40C as an ice room fan error.
  • 83E: A power or voltage problem. Samsung lists 83E as abnormal compressor current, 85E as compressor under voltage, 86E as compressor over voltage, and 88 88 as a communication fault during boot.
