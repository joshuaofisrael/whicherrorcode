LG washer code UE

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: The load is piled on one side, or it is too large or too small, so the drum cannot balance.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Do not machine wash items LG lists as unsuitable, including bathroom mats, carpets, electric blankets, curtains, rubber mats, waterproof clothes, and dolls.
  • Keep the drum about two thirds full. Pause and spread the laundry evenly.
  • If the washer rocks, level the feet so each foot touches the floor.

When to call a technician

If a normal mixed load still will not spin after it is leveled, request service.

More LG washer codes

  • OE: The washer cannot drain. LG says a kinked drain hose or a clogged drain pump filter can cause it.
  • IE: The tub does not fill in the time LG allows. The faucet may be off, the hose kinked or frozen, or the hose not connected.
  • dE: The door did not lock. dE or dE1 means the door is not closed properly. dE2 means the door is closed but not locked.
  • LE: The tub does not spin. LG says the motor cannot run because the washer is overloaded or a foreign object is stuck.
  • Sud: Too many suds. The washer adds a suds reducing cycle, which LG says can add about 2 hours. If suds are detected during spin, it stops to help prevent leaking.
  • FE: Water overfill. LG says the drain pump runs to help prevent leaks, and extra water may be entering through the detergent dispenser.
