LG washer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the LG washer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

OE

The washer cannot drain. LG says a kinked drain hose or a clogged drain pump filter can cause it. Also listed as E03, E21. Check behind the washer and straighten or clear the drain hose, then run a spin cycle. If the hose is straight, run a spin only cycle and listen for the drain pump. LG's spin only steps are: power on, press Spin Speed until High, then Start. If you hear the pump, clean the drain pump filter. LG says to keep the drain tube cap tight so it does not leak, and to run Tub Clean if suds were the cause. Use less detergent after that. If OE returns, LG says the washer may need repair. Full page for OE.

IE

The tub does not fill in the time LG allows. The faucet may be off, the hose kinked or frozen, or the hose not connected. Also listed as E02, E10. Confirm the supply hose is connected and the faucet is open. Straighten the hose. If it is badly bent, turn the faucet off before you disconnect it. In cold weather, thaw a frozen faucet or hose with warm water. LG says 50 degrees C or lower for the hose, not boiling water. Turn the faucet off, remove the inlet filter, clear rust or sand, and put the filter back before reconnecting. If those checks fail, LG says the water supply valve may need service. Full page for IE.

UE

The load is piled on one side, or it is too large or too small, so the drum cannot balance. Do not machine wash items LG lists as unsuitable, including bathroom mats, carpets, electric blankets, curtains, rubber mats, waterproof clothes, and dolls. Keep the drum about two thirds full. Pause and spread the laundry evenly. If the washer rocks, level the feet so each foot touches the floor. If a normal mixed load still will not spin after it is leveled, request service. Full page for UE.

dE

The door did not lock. dE or dE1 means the door is not closed properly. dE2 means the door is closed but not locked. Also listed as dE1, E01, dE2, E30. Remove laundry caught in the gasket, then close the door again. Power the washer off, unplug it or switch off the breaker, hold Start/Pause for 5 seconds, restore power, and try again. If dE1 or dE2 continues, LG says service is required. A bent or broken latch is a service item. Full page for dE.

LE

The tub does not spin. LG says the motor cannot run because the washer is overloaded or a foreign object is stuck. Unplug the washer, wait 5 minutes, and try the cycle again. Reduce the load. Use the bedding cycle for a heavy load or a large blanket. With power off and the tub empty, remove a coin or other object stuck in the tub. If the code continues, unplug the washer and contact LG service. Full page for LE.

Sud

Too many suds. The washer adds a suds reducing cycle, which LG says can add about 2 hours. If suds are detected during spin, it stops to help prevent leaking. Use HE detergent and do not pass the amount on the detergent label. Put liquid detergent only to the Max line, with the liquid cup in place. Remove that cup when you use powder. Put one pod in the drum, not in the drawer. This is a detergent problem, not a failed pump, unless the code continues after you reduce detergent. Full page for Sud.

FE

Water overfill. LG says the drain pump runs to help prevent leaks, and extra water may be entering through the detergent dispenser. Also listed as E04, E12. Check whether extra water is coming in through the dispenser. Unplug the washer, wait 60 seconds, and restart. Start a Quick 30 program. LG says it should start with a drain. If it does not, stop the program and turn the washer off. If FE remains, LG says the washer needs inspection. Full page for FE.

tE

A heating error, which LG says is usually a faulty thermistor. Unplug the washer for 10 seconds, plug it back in, and set spin speed to no spin so it can drain, then restart the wash. If tE remains, close the water taps, unplug the washer, and request service. Full page for tE.

AE

A leak inside the appliance. LG says a safety switch is there to help prevent flooding. Check the inlet hose for a loose connection or a cut. Unplug the washer, wait 60 seconds, and restart. If it persists, shut off the water and schedule service. LG lists a faulty safety switch as one cause, which is not an owner repair.

PE

The water level sensor is not working correctly. Unplug the washer for 10 seconds, plug it back in, and restart the cycle. If PE remains, LG says the washer needs repair.

FF

Freeze failure. LG says this can occur when the inside of the washer drops below 1 degree C. Controls stay disabled until the machine is above 4 degrees C. If the weather is cold, thaw the tub and drain hose with warm water. LG says 50 to 60 degrees C in the drum, not boiling water, then wait 1 to 2 hours. Drain residual water from the lower drain tube and close the cap fully. Thaw inlet hoses below 50 degrees C and confirm the taps flow before reconnecting. If the code appears when the weather is not cold, LG says to request service.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these LG washer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the LG support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does OE mean the same thing on every LG washer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a LG washer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
