LG oven error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

These are the LG oven codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

This appliance may burn gas. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published checks.

F1

LG lists F1 as an upper oven thermistor error. The same range error page gives F2 the same upper oven thermistor meaning. Also listed as F2. Press the CLEAR/OFF key and try the original function again. If the error code reappears, turn the circuit breaker to the unit off and wait 30 seconds before turning it back on. If the problem continues or the troubleshooting steps cannot be performed, LG says the unit will require service. On a gas range, shut the gas off before any inspection past these steps. H5

Not a fault. LG lists H5 for a cooktop surface hotter than 150 F (the page also writes HS). It is only an indication that the cooktop is too hot to touch. Also listed as HS. If the surface is already at room temperature and the indicator does not turn off, turn the circuit breaker to the unit off and wait 30 seconds before turning it back on. If the hot surface indicator stays on when the cooktop is cool, LG says the unit will require service. Full page for H5.

Sb

Not a fault. The unit is in Sabbath Mode. While active, the temperature cannot be changed and the timer, light, and alarm functions are disabled. If the oven light was on when Sabbath Mode started, it stays on. Also listed as 5b. Press Bake. Set the Bake temperature using the + and - keys. Press Start. Press and hold Clock for 3 seconds. No service call is indicated for Sabbath Mode by itself. If those steps do not clear it, contact LG support. Full page for Sb.

LOC

Not a fault. LG's range power page says OVEN LOCKOUT or CHILD LOCK means the control panel is locked. Also listed as Oven Lockout, Child Lock. Press and hold either START or the LOCK key for 3 seconds to deactivate the lockout feature. Also confirm Delay Start is not active. DELAY START on the display means the cycle is waiting and will not run immediately. No service call is indicated for lockout by itself. If START or LOCK for 3 seconds does not clear it, contact LG support. Full page for LOC.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these LG oven codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the LG support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does F1 mean the same thing on every LG oven?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a LG oven?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
