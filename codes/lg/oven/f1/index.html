LG oven code F1

By Which Error Code. Last verified 19 Nov 2026.

Meaning: LG lists F1 as an upper oven thermistor error. The same range error page gives F2 the same upper oven thermistor meaning.

The same manufacturer page also lists F2.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Press the CLEAR/OFF key and try the original function again.
  • If the error code reappears, turn the circuit breaker to the unit off and wait 30 seconds before turning it back on.

When to call a technician

If the problem continues or the troubleshooting steps cannot be performed, LG says the unit will require service. On a gas range, shut the gas off before any inspection past these steps.

More LG oven codes

  • F3: Touch pad sensor key error.
  • F10: Door locking system failure on the upper oven.
  • PF: Power has been interrupted to the appliance.
  • H5: Not a fault. LG lists H5 for a cooktop surface hotter than 150 F (the page also writes HS). It is only an indication that the cooktop is too hot to touch.
  • Sb: Not a fault. The unit is in Sabbath Mode. While active, the temperature cannot be changed and the timer, light, and alarm functions are disabled. If the oven light was on when Sabbath Mode started, it stays on.
  • LOC: Not a fault. LG's range power page says OVEN LOCKOUT or CHILD LOCK means the control panel is locked.
