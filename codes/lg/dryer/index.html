LG dryer error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the LG dryer codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

This appliance may burn gas. Shut the gas off before any inspection past the published checks.

tE1

A problem was detected with the thermistor. Also listed as tE2, tE3, tE4, tE5, tE6. Unplug the dryer or trip the breaker. Hold Start/Pause for 5 seconds while power is off. Restore power and run another cycle. LG also describes a separate tE2 during steam cycles in cold conditions. For that case, clean the lint tray and check the outside exhaust flappers. If tE returns after the reset, LG says the dryer may need repair. Full page for tE1.

d80

Airflow is restricted between the dryer and the outside exhaust. Also listed as d90, d95. Remove lint from the lint tray between loads. Wash the tray with warm water and a soft brush and let it dry before you put it back. Use a smaller load. Behind the dryer, confirm the exhaust hose is not crushed, kinked, coiled, torn, or loose. Outside, confirm the flapper opens fully and clear visible lint. LG says an Air Dry cycle should open the flapper. If those steps fail, LG says to hire a dryer duct cleaner for the vent inside the walls. Give them the duct length and the number of 90 degree turns. Full page for d80.

dE

The door is not completely closed. Also listed as dE4. Open the door, push the filter all the way in, and remove clothing stuck on the door. Unplug the dryer or trip the breaker, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in, close the door firmly, and start a cycle. If the code returns, LG says the dryer needs service. A bent new door should go back to the seller. Full page for dE.

gAS

The dryer is not receiving gas. LG's published check is to make sure the gas supply valve is turned on and the gas line is not kinked. Do not open the burner or loosen a gas fitting. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility. If the valve is on, the line is not kinked, and you do not smell gas, but gAS remains, request service. Full page for gAS.

nP

No current is detected at the heater. LG says this normally indicates a problem with the home wiring. Confirm the cord is plugged into a grounded outlet that matches the 240 volt rating plate. Switch the dryer breaker off for at least 10 seconds, then on. Have a licensed electrician check the outlet if the code remains. If the dryer still does not heat, LG says it needs repair. This code is about electric heat, not a gas valve. Full page for nP.

drn

On a dryer, LG uses drn or OE when the water container is full, the drain hose is kinked, or the drain pump is air locked. This is not the washer OE code. Also listed as OE. Empty the water container and push it in until it clicks. Straighten the drain hose. LG says the top of the hose should be no more than 1 meter above the floor, and the end should not sit under water. To clear residual water, LG says to run Air Dry for 2 to 3 minutes and then power off. Some models also have a drain hose cap. Close that cap firmly. If the container is empty and the hose is clear but the code returns, or the dryer stops again after 10 to 20 minutes, LG says to request service. Full page for drn.

PS

Improper voltage at the power cord. LG says the white and red wires on the terminal block may be reversed. Check the cord installation against LG's 4 wire or 3 wire installation steps. Do not guess at the terminal block. Reset the breaker for at least 10 seconds and confirm a grounded 240 volt outlet. If the dryer still will not heat, LG says it needs repair. Have a qualified person correct the cord.

LE1

LE1 means the drum is not rotating because of the drum motor. LE2, AE, or CE1 means a compressor problem. LE3 means the motor that circulates air. Also listed as LE2, LE3, AE, CE1. For LE1, remove part of a heavy load and try again. For LE2, AE, CE1, or LE3, unplug the dryer, wait one minute, reconnect, and try again. If the code remains, LG says to request service. LG says LE3 cannot be fixed with simple troubleshooting.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these LG dryer codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the LG support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does tE1 mean the same thing on every LG dryer?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a LG dryer?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
