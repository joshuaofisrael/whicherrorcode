LG dryer code dE

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: The door is not completely closed.

The same manufacturer page also lists dE4.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Open the door, push the filter all the way in, and remove clothing stuck on the door.
  • Unplug the dryer or trip the breaker, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in, close the door firmly, and start a cycle.

When to call a technician

If the code returns, LG says the dryer needs service. A bent new door should go back to the seller.

More LG dryer codes

  • tE1: A problem was detected with the thermistor.
  • d80: Airflow is restricted between the dryer and the outside exhaust.
  • gAS: The dryer is not receiving gas.
  • nP: No current is detected at the heater. LG says this normally indicates a problem with the home wiring.
  • drn: On a dryer, LG uses drn or OE when the water container is full, the drain hose is kinked, or the drain pump is air locked. This is not the washer OE code.
