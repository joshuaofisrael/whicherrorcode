LG dishwasher error codes

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

These are the LG dishwasher codes we could verify on the manufacturer's own page. Each block says what the code means, the checks that page allows, and when it says to call a technician.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

IE

Water is not supplied to the tub. LG calls this a water inlet error. Confirm the kitchen taps have water and the inlet hose is not pressed or kinked. If the dishwasher was just installed, open the inlet valve and the shutoff under the sink by turning them counterclockwise. If the valves are open and the hose is straight but IE remains, LG says a technician is required. Full page for IE.

OE

The tub is not fully drained. LG calls this a water outlet error. Clean the filter. LG says about once every one or two weeks. Wait 30 minutes after a cycle so the tub is not hot. Remove the lower rack, turn the filter handle counterclockwise, separate the inner filter and the stainless filter, wash both, and turn the handle clockwise until it clicks. Close the shutoff before you move the drain hose, then straighten any kink. LG says the end of the drain hose should be at least 30 cm above the floor. Standing water below the filter is normal, LG says, because it traps sewer smells. If water stays above the filter, request service. Full page for OE.

AE

LG groups FE, AE, and RE as too much water, a leak, oversudsing from dishwashing liquid, a unit that is not level, a door that is not aligned, or water poured into the Heat Dry blower. Also listed as FE, RE. Load large plates and pots upside down. Use detergent made for dishwashers. Do not use dishwashing liquid. Do not pull out a built in dishwasher if you poured water into the Heat Dry blower. LG says to request service so the floor is not damaged. The dishwasher may keep draining. If no water is on the floor but the code remains, unplug it or switch off its power and request service. If the cabinet is not level, or the code remains after the detergent check, LG says to request service. Full page for AE.

bE

Bubble error. LG says oversudsing from dish soap or too much detergent can cause it, and the code is there to help prevent leaks. Do not use dish soap. Use powdered or solid dishwasher detergent, or only a small amount of liquid dishwasher detergent. Run the dishwasher empty with no detergent. If suds and bE appear, the detergent was the cause. LG's suds clearing steps: set rinse aid to L0, add no detergent, place a bowl with 200 ml (7 oz) of milk in the middle of the upper rack, and run a Standard cycle with Extra Dry and Rinse. Repeat if suds remain, then put rinse aid back. If bE appears when there are no suds, LG says to request service. Full page for bE.

HE

The heater that heats the wash water is not working. LG does not publish an owner repair. Do not open the heater. Request service. Full page for HE.

tE

A temperature sensor problem, such as a contact problem or a failed sensor. LG does not publish an owner repair for tE. Request service.

LE

The cleaning motor is not working normally. LG does not publish an owner repair for LE. Request service.

nE

The impeller is not spinning normally. Dishes may be touching it. Reorganize the dishes so nothing touches the impeller, and restart. If nE remains, LG says a technician is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these LG dishwasher codes come from?

Each code on this page is taken from the LG support page linked in Sources. We do not add codes from repair blogs or from another brand.

Does IE mean the same thing on every LG dishwasher?

No. The manufacturer often says a code applies to some models only, or that the same letters mean something else on another model. Check the manual for your model number.

What should I do before I open a panel on a LG dishwasher?

Unplug it or switch off the breaker. If it burns gas, shut the gas supply off before any step the manufacturer did not publish. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility.
