LG refrigerator code dH

By Which Error Code. Last verified 16 Nov 2026.

Meaning: Defrost problem. LG says the defrost sensor temperature has not risen by more than 5 degrees C within 80 minutes of defrost starting. The evaporator may be blocked by ice.

The same manufacturer page also lists FdH, dHF, Er dH.

Safety before any check

Unplug the appliance or switch off its breaker before you move it, open a panel, or clean a filter. If it uses gas, shut the gas supply off before you go past the checks the manufacturer published. Do not open a gas valve, an igniter, or a gas line.

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services. If water is reaching the floor, shut the supply valve off. Stop and call a technician when the code returns, when the manufacturer says service is required, or when the check is not on the cited page.

Checks the manufacturer publishes

  • Use the 5 minute unplug reset from LG's error page.
  • Do not chip ice out of a sealed evaporator.

When to call a technician

If dH remains, request service.

More LG refrigerator codes

  • CF: Condenser fan fault. LG says this fan cools the condenser. If it is blocked, the compressor works harder and the inside gets warmer.
  • FF: Freezer fan fault. LG says this fan moves cold air through the freezer. A blocked fan makes the compressor work harder.
  • IF: LG includes Er IF in the group of internal component codes. The same family of pages treats ice maker sensor problems separately as IS.
  • gF: Flow sensor fault. LG says it can be a closed water tap, a kinked hose, or incorrect water pressure.
